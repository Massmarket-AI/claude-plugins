What this extension records

While you are signed in on this browser profile, this extension records the web address and page title of the tab you are working in, how long it is active or idle, and how many times you clicked, typed, scrolled or pasted in each minute. It never records which keys you pressed, what you typed, or what you copied. It records nothing while you are signed out or paused, and it cannot see other apps, other browser profiles, or your screen outside this profile. It takes no screenshots, except during a QA capture you start yourself, which saves one per click until you finish. While a timer is running, it also records the network location (IP address and city) you connect from.
